What is InternNova?

InternNova is an online platform that gives students practical experience in technology. Instead of only watching lectures, students complete tasks, build projects and get guidance from mentors.

Every program runs online, so a student from any college or city can join.

Who is it for?

  • College students from different branches and universities
  • Students who want hands-on practice, not only theory
  • Students who need project work and a certificate for their resume

Learning and mentorship

Every program follows the same simple path. You always know what to do next and who to ask when you are stuck.

  • A student dashboard for your tasks and learning resources
  • Mentor feedback on the work you submit
  • A final evaluation before you receive your certificate
  1. Apply and enroll

    Pick your domain and complete enrollment to reserve a seat in the next batch.

  2. Open your dashboard

    Log in to see your weekly tasks and learning resources.

  3. Build projects

    Complete tasks and projects with guidance from mentors.

  4. Get certified

    Submit your final evaluation to receive your verified certificate.
